# User Stories

- Data Visualization & Analysis
  Interactive Spending Breakdown: As a visual learner, I want an attractive donut-style pie chart of my monthly expenses so that I can tap on slices like "Dining" to drill down into specific transactions.

Savings vs. Spending Ratio: As a goal-oriented user, I want to see my total monthly savings displayed in the center of my expense pie chart, so I can immediately see the balance between what I’ve kept and what I’ve spent.

Monthly Momentum: As an analytical user, I want to see a summary of how many targets I set this month compared to how much I actually saved, so I can evaluate if my ambition matches my financial reality.

- Goal Setting & Targets
  Multi-Target Management: As a dreamer with many plans, I want to set multiple savings targets (e.g.,"Summer Trip") simultaneously and see a progress bar for each to stay motivated.

Target Feasibility: As a realistic planner, I want to see a "Success Forecast" for my targets that adjusts in real-time based on my current monthly expense data.

- AI Agent Advisor
  Smart Spending Intervention: As an impulsive spender, I want the AI Agent to analyze my pie chart and proactively suggest which "slice" I should shrink to hit my savings target 10% faster.

Natural Language Inquiry: As a non-technical user, I want to ask the AI, "How much have I saved toward my New Car target this month?" and receive a concise, conversational summary of my progress.

Automated Audit: As a busy professional, I want the AI to flag when my monthly expenses in a specific category (like "Utilities") spike unexpectedly compared to my 6-month average.

- The Anonymous Leaderboard
  Family Challenge: As a head of household, I want to create a private family leaderboard so that my spouse and teenage children can compete anonymously (using avatars) to see who hits their individual savings targets first.

Savings Rate Equality (Global): As a user with a modest income, I want the Global Leaderboard to rank users by "Savings Percentage Rate" (e.g., saving 20% of income) rather than total dollars, so I can fairly compete against high-earners.

Privacy-First Avatars: As a private individual, I want to choose a funny pseudonym and avatar for the leaderboards so that I can enjoy the competition without anyone knowing my actual identity or bank balance.
